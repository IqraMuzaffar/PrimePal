# HepaBot LinkedIn Post

Late to share -- our team secured 3rd Position in the Large Language Models (LLM) course project at LUMS!

We built HepaBot -- a clinical AI assistant for liver disease diagnosis and decision support, using real patient data from PKLI (Pakistan Kidney & Liver Institute).

--

The Problem?

Liver disease is one of Pakistan's biggest health challenges. At institutions like PKLI, clinicians handle a high volume of hepatology cases daily. Doctor-patient conversations contain critical clinical information -- but it remains unstructured under audio recordings and handwritten notes. Clinicians spend valuable time manually reviewing patient histories, lab results, and cross-referencing clinical guidelines like AASLD to reach a diagnosis.

There's no efficient way to extract diagnostic insights from these conversations and turn them into actionable clinical reports.

--

What is HepaBot?

A domain-specific clinical AI system that transcribes, analyzes, and structures liver-related patient conversations into actionable clinical reports.

--

What it does:

1. AI-Powered Diagnosis
Enter patient symptoms, lab results, and clinical history -- HepaBot analyzes and suggests diagnoses with detailed clinical reasoning

2. RAG Pipeline with AASLD Guidelines
Embedded American Association for the Study of Liver Diseases clinical guidelines into a vector database (ChromaDB) for evidence-based retrieval

3. Clinical Report Generation
Record or upload doctor-patient conversations (audio/PDF) and auto-generate structured medical reports -- Clinical Summary, Diagnostic Information, Treatment Plans, and Diagnostic Evidence

4. Fine-tuned Clinical BERT (LoRA, rank 4)
Domain-specific understanding on our PKLI dataset + GPT-4o-mini for structured report generation

--

This project reinforced how AI can meaningfully support clinical decision-making -- not replace clinicians, but give them structured, evidence-backed insights faster.

Grateful for the recognition and the opportunity to work with real-world clinical data from PKLI!
