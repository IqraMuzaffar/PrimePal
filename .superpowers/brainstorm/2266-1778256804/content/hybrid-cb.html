What's "B" in this hybrid:

  • Saturated card gradients — purple, pink, amber, cyan, emerald (not the muted pastel cards from pure C).
  • Glow blobs behind the hero (radial sun + lavender bloom).
  • Animated mascot + waving hand for energy.

What stays "C":

  • Pastel hero gradient (pink → lavender → mint).
  • Soft cream + lavender + cyan page background (no harsh white).
  • Generous spacing & rounded-2xl/3xl corners — calm, not chaotic.
  • Light navbar (not a dark bar like today).

Animations included:

  • ⭐ logo: slow rotate · 👋 hand: wave · 🦄 mascot: float · 🚀: bounce
  • Hero glow blobs: pulse · Progress bar: shimmer
  • Cards: stagger-in on load, lift+slight-rotate on hover, blob expands, icon scale-rotate
  • NEW badge: pulse · Badges: lift & icon wiggle on hover

Hover the cards / badges in the preview to feel the motion. Tell me: love it / tone down / more punch, or what to change.
