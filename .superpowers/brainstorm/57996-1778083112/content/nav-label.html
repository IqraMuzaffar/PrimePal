What should the navbar item be called?

This label appears in the left sidebar navigation alongside Dashboard, Classrooms, Students, etc.

A

Analytics

Short, standard. Immediately understood as aggregate/class-level data.

B

Class Analytics

Explicitly scoped to class/grade level. Distinguishes it clearly from individual Reports.

C

Insights

Softer, more modern feel. Works well alongside "Reports" which is more formal/structured.

D

Overview

Suggests a bird's-eye view of all grades/sections — pairs nicely with Reports for detail.
