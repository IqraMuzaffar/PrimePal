Which color direction feels right for PrimePal?

The current landing page uses indigo/violet/purple gradients. Let's explore better options that align with the brand's teacher/admin login pages.

A

Professional Blue — Modern & Trustworthy

Matches your teacher/admin login pages. Deep navy background with bright blue accents. Professional, educational, trustworthy.

  • Consistent with existing design
  • Professional & credible
  • Great for educational context
  • Less playful for kids
  • Common in ed-tech
B

Vibrant Gradient — Fun & Energetic

Bold, colorful gradients that appeal to children. Purple-pink-blue spectrum creates excitement and playfulness.

  • Eye-catching for kids
  • Feels modern & fun
  • Stands out from competitors
  • May feel too casual
  • Different from login pages
C

Soft & Friendly — Balanced Approach

Softer blue-cyan palette. Friendly and approachable while maintaining professionalism. Bridge between playful and credible.

  • Appeals to both kids & educators
  • Modern but not overwhelming
  • Versatile for all ages
  • Less distinctive
  • Might feel "safe"
D

Warm & Welcoming — Orange/Teal Contrast

Warm orange (like your current student card!) paired with cool teal. High contrast, energetic, and memorable.

  • Already using orange for student card
  • High energy & warmth
  • Unique in ed-tech space
  • Bold departure from login pages
  • Harder to maintain consistency

Note: Your teacher/admin login pages currently use deep navy (#0b1535) with bright blue (#4361ee) accents. Option A matches this exactly. Options B-D would create visual variety but less consistency.
